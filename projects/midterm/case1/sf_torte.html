Chicken Adobo

by Panlasang Pinoy

The iconic national Filipino Dish of the Philippines, featuring bone-in chicken braised in a savory, tangy, and garlicky sauce made from soy sauce, vinegar, garlic, bay leaves, and black peppercorns..

Ingredients

  • 2 lbs Chicken cut into serving pieces
  • 3 pieces dried bay leaves
  • 4 tablespoons soy sauce
  • 4 1/2 tablespoons white vinegar
  • 10 cloves garlic crushed
  • 1 1/2 cups water
  • 4 tablespoons cooking oil
  • 1 teaspoon sugar
  • 1 teaspoon whole peppercorn
  • Salt to taste

Directions

  1. Crush the garlic using a mortar and pestle: 10 cloves garlic
  2. Combine the chicken, soy sauce, and crushed garlic in a large bowl. Mix well and marinate for at least 1 hour. The longer the chicken marinates, the better the flavor: 2 lbs chicken, 4 tablespoons soy sauce, 10 cloves garlic
  3. Remove the chicken pieces from the marinade using kitchen tongs. Scoop out the crushed garlic with a slotted spoon. Reserve the garlic and marinade separately: 2 lbs chicken, 4 tablespoons soy sauce, 10 cloves garlic
  4. Heat a cooking pot and pour in 3 tablespoons of cooking oil. 4 tablespoons cooking oil
  5. Once the oil gets hot, pan-fry the marinated chicken for 2 minutes per side. 2 lbs chicken
  6. Remove the chicken from the pot and set it aside: 2 lbs chicken
  7. Pour the remaining cooking oil into the same pot: 4 tablespoons cooking oil
  8. Add the garlic from the marinade and sauté until it starts to brown. 10 cloves garlic
  9. Return the chicken to the pot. 2 lbs chicken
  10. Add the remaining marinade, whole peppercorns, and dried bay leaves. 3 pieces dried bay leaves, 4 tablespoons soy sauce, 1 teaspoon whole peppercorn
  11. Pour in the water and let it boil. 1 1/2 cups water
  12. Cover the pot and simmer for 30 minutes, or until the chicken becomes tender. 2 lbs chicken
  13. Pour in the vinegar. Let it boil for 1 minute without stirring. Stir, then continue simmering uncovered for 15 minutes. 4 1/2 tablespoons white vinegar
  14. Add the sugar and salt as needed. Stir, then turn the heat off. 1 teaspoon sugar, Salt
  15. Transfer to a serving plate. Serve hot with rice. Share and enjoy!
